🍝 Creamy Garlic Pasta

A simple and delicious creamy garlic pasta that is perfect for lunch or dinner. This recipe is easy to follow and can be prepared in less than 30 minutes.

About This Recipe

Creamy garlic pasta is a comforting dish made with pasta, garlic, cream, and Parmesan cheese. It has a rich and smooth texture with a delicious garlic flavour.

Preparation Time: 10 minutes
Cooking Time: 20 minutes
Total Time: 30 minutes
Servings: 2 people

What You Need

Main Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti or fettuccine
  • 4 cloves of fresh garlic
  • 1 cup cooking cream
  • 50g Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley

Optional Ingredients

  • Chilli flakes
  • Grilled chicken
  • Mushrooms
  • Cherry tomatoes

Steps

  1. Fill a large pot with water and bring it to a boil. Add some salt to the water.
  2. Add the pasta and cook it according to the instructions on the package until it is al dente.
  3. While the pasta is cooking, peel and finely chop the garlic.
  4. Heat butter and olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat.
  5. Add the chopped garlic and cook for about one minute. Do not burn the garlic because it can become bitter.
  6. Pour the cooking cream into the pan and stir everything together.
  7. Add Parmesan cheese, salt, and black pepper. Continue stirring until the sauce becomes thick and creamy.
  8. Drain the pasta but save a small amount of the pasta water.
  9. Add the cooked pasta to the creamy sauce and mix well. If the sauce is too thick, add a little pasta water.
  10. For the best flavour, let the pasta rest for one minute before serving.
  11. Add chopped parsley and optional chilli flakes on top.

Serving Suggestion

Serve the pasta immediately while it is warm and creamy. You can add extra Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley before serving.

Chef's Tips

  • Use fresh garlic for a stronger flavour.
  • Do not overcook the pasta.
  • Keep some pasta water before draining.
  • Use freshly grated Parmesan for a smoother sauce.
  • Serve the pasta immediately for the best taste.
